## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Overview

**BEZ/8 Cafe** is a vibrant, modern fast food, specialty juice, and cafe destination located in the Kolfe Likwanda district of Addis Ababa. Opened on **November 23, 2023** (*ኅዳር 13፣ 2016 ዓ.ም.*), BEZ/8 has become a beloved neighborhood culinary anchor known for its warm Ethiopian hospitality, energetic contemporary interior aesthetics, and commitment to serving 100% fresh, made-to-order food and refreshing beverages.

The physical space is immediately recognizable by its signature yellow honeycomb wall designs, lush green indoor moss/grass feature wall crowned with the neon **"BEZ/8 JUICE & FAST FOOD"** emblem, colorful pop-art murals, and dedicated artisanal espresso bar.

## 3. Brand Identity & Visual Language System

### 🎨 Color Palette
- **Deep Obsidian Black (`#111113`)**: Primary backdrop and structural accents, establishing a sleek modern night-cafe mood.
- **Vibrant Neon / Amber Yellow (`#FACC15` / `#EAB308`)**: The hallmark color of the official BEZ/8 logo, yellow leather booths, and honeycomb wall pattern.
- **Crimson Brand Red (`#DC2626` / `#EF4444`)**: Energetic accent representing fresh fast food, pizza sauce, and hospitality warmth.
- **Botanical Moss Green (`#16A34A` / `#22C55E`)**: Directly inspired by the iconic living moss feature wall inside the cafe.
- **Surface Elevation Cards (`#1C1917` / `#27272A`)**: Tactile card containers with soft borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Crisp High-Contrast Text (`#FFFFFF` & `#E5E7EB`)**: Maximum legibility on mobile screens under direct sunlight.

### 🔤 Typography System
- **Ethiopic Display & Body**: `Noto Sans Ethiopic` & `Noto Serif Ethiopic` — *Culturally authentic, legible Geez rendering across all screen sizes*.
- **Modern Sans (English)**: `Outfit` & `Plus Jakarta Sans` — *Crisp geometric clarity that complements the honeycomb modern interior*.
